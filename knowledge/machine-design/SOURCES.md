The GD&T decks are teaching material. Select the governing ASME or ISO system and
edition for a drawing; use the applicable standards for contractual definitions
and fit designations. This bundle does not reproduce standards tables, certify
compliance or provide material allowables.

The numerical fit example and the noBS CAD workflows are original teaching
examples. Their dimensions are not manufacturing recommendations.
