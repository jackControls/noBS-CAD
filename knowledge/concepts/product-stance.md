# Product stance

noBS CAD is **local-first, free, open-source mechanical CAD**.

- Project and modeling data stay on the user's computer.
- No account, subscription, or cloud backend.
- Sketch-and-extrude workflow first; parametric history.
- Built and operated with AI agents via a first-class **MCP harness**, without turning the product into a cloud CAD.

Related: [Architecture](architecture.md), [MCP harness](mcp-harness.md).
